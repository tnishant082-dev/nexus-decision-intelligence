# Vendor SLA & Risk Policy (SAMPLE)

> **Sample policy for NEXUS RAG demos.**

## Preferred vendor mix
Target preferred-vendor PO value share ≥ 75%.

## SLA
- Standard receipt SLA: 4 business days from PO promise date.
- On-time receipt below 80% for two consecutive months → performance plan.

## Risk tiers
| Tier | Action |
|---|---|
| Low | Standard monitoring |
| Medium | Monthly scorecard |
| High | Dual-source plan + quality holds |

## Quality holds
Fraud / cancel spikes > 2× category baseline trigger receiving inspection.
