# Freight & Expedite Policy (SAMPLE)

> **Sample policy for NEXUS RAG demos.**

## Mode selection
Prefer ground when SLA allows. Air only when:
- Customer SLA breach risk within 48h, or
- A-class stockout with revenue impact > threshold.

## Expedite share
Network expedite share target < 12%. Above 20% for a rolling 4 weeks → logistics deep-dive.

## CO2
Track CO2 kg per shipment. Air share above 20% of shipments requires sustainability note in monthly ops review.
